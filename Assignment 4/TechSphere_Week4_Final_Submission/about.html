Our History

Why Fixora was started.

Fixora was founded in 2022 after its fictional founders noticed how difficult it could be for everyday customers to understand a phone repair quote. The business was designed around a clearer experience: diagnose first, explain the options, then repair only what the customer approves.

2022

Founded

Fixora opens as a small repair desk focused on screen and battery jobs.

2024

Expanded

Software support and quality-check routines become part of the service.

2026

Customer-first

The concept grows into a full-service local repair studio experience.
